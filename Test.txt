  1. The Brewing Cycle (How long it takes)
  At the start of the fight (and whenever her "deck" is empty), she enters a mandatory brewing state. She stops moving completely during this time.

   * Choice of what to brew: She picks 3 potions at random from the full pool: [POISON, FIRE, HEALING, SHADOW, BLOOD, HOLY].
   * Brewing Windup: She starts with a "Windup" of 60 frames (1 second). At the end of this second, she triggers a Shockwave that stuns you if you are too close.
   * Brewing Duration: Each of the 3 potions takes 120 frames (2 seconds) to brew.
   * Total Downtime: It takes exactly 7 seconds (1s windup + 3 potions x 2s) for her to fully reload her deck. During these 7 seconds, she is a "sitting duck" but takes 50%
     less damage (if self.brewing_active: amount *= 0.5).

  2. Movement (How she moves)
  In Phase 1, her movement is highly predictable but defensive:
   * Orbiting: She maintains a strict circular orbit around you at a radius of 4.5 units (ORBIT_RADIUS).
   * Speed: She moves at a base speed of 0.06 units/frame.
   * Blinking: If you get too close (within 2.5 units), she will immediately "Vanish" (Blink) to the opposite side of the arena to maintain her distance.

  3. Throwing Timing (Time between potions)
  Once she has her 3 potions ready, she begins her attack phase:
   * Interval: In Phase 1, she throws one potion every 3 seconds (THROW_TIMINGS[0] is 3.0s).
   * The Telegraph: 0.4 seconds before the throw, she stops moving, and a colored icon/glow appears above her head to tell you what she’s about to use.

  4. The Decision Tree (The "Choice" Logic)

  Which one does she pick to throw?
  She doesn't follow a specific order. She picks a random index from her current list of 3 brewed potions. If she has [FIRE, HEALING, POISON], she might throw the POISON first
  or the FIRE first.

  Does she Drink or Throw?
  Once she has picked a potion from her hand, she runs this check:

   1. HEALING:
       * Drink: If her HP is below 60%.
       * Throw: If her HP is above 60% (she throws it at you, but it only creates a "Heal Zone" on the floor that she can stand in later).
   2. SHADOW:
       * Drink (50% chance): She turns invisible and tries to hit you with a melee "Assassin" strike.
       * Throw (50% chance): She creates a "Shadow Zone" that blinds you if you enter it.
   3. BLOOD:
       * Drink (50% chance): She casts a defensive ring of spikes around herself.
       * Throw (50% chance): She fires a homing blood projectile at you.
   4. FIRE / POISON / HOLY:
       * Drink (20% chance): She takes a small buff (Fire empowerment, Poison skin, or Holy shield).
       * Throw (80% chance): She throws it as a standard AOE hazard to control where you can walk.

  Summary of Phase 1 Flow:
   1. Wait 1s (Shockwave at the end).
   2. Wait 6s (Brews 3 random potions while stationary).
   3. Move & Attack:
       * Throw/Drink Potion 1.
       * Wait 3s.
       * Throw/Drink Potion 2.
       * Wait 3s.
       * Throw/Drink Potion 3.
   4. Repeat (Return to Step 1).
